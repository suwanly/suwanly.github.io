## About Me

I'm **Suwan Yoon**, an undergraduate student at **Chung-Ang University** and a **research intern** at **Korea University's Actionable Intelligence Laboratory** led by Prof. **Changhee Lee**.

My work centers on:
- **Generative Models**
- **Large Language Models**
- **Time-Series Analysis**
- **Healthcare AI**

I also enjoy exploring emerging areas, such as **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **Knowledge Distillation (KD)**, **Optimal Transport (OT)**, and **Vision-Language Models (VLM)**.
